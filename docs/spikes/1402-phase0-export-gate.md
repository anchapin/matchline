identical). A manifest over IFC4 is only viable if the IFC4 is normalised (timestamp stripped,
GlobalIds canonicalised) before hashing, or if the manifest carries a *semantic* digest of the IFC4
instead of a file hash.

## 2. Retrospective geometric audit of today's exports

Throwaway script `/tmp/1402/geom_audit.py` parses the **published gbXML** (`Space/ShellGeometry/ClosedShell/PolyLoop`,
namespace `http://www.gbxml.org/schema`, points in `<Coordinate>` children) and implements the Part A
checks with the starting tolerances from the brief:

* **closed shell** — every undirected edge of a space shell is used by exactly **2** faces, and the two
  faces traverse it in **opposite** directions (direction check = winding consistency);
* **signed volume** — divergence-theorem volume of the oriented shell must be **> 0**;
* **planarity** — every vertex of a face must lie within **1e-3 m** of the face's best-fit plane;
  the **warped spread** (max−min signed deviation of a face) is checked against **9e-4 m**;
* **degenerate face** — area ≤ 1e-9 m² or fewer than 3 distinct vertices;
* **duplicate face** — two faces with the same vertex set.

Two caveats measured here, both relevant to how Part A must be written:

1. **The published `<Volume>` is not the shell volume.** `bem/bem_export.py:469` writes
   `sp.volume_m3` (= area × wall height) for the flat-roof path, while `shell_volume(loops)` (the
   divergence-theorem value of the *published* shell) is used only on the sloped-roof path

   quantities — a Part A volume check must compute the volume **from the published shell**, not trust
   the `<Volume>` element.
2. **The audit covers the gbXML space shells only.** The IFC4 side encodes each space as an extruded
   solid (`IfcSpace` `Body`/`SweptSolid`), so there is no per-face shell to audit there; if the gate
   must cover the IFC4 artifact too, it needs a *cross-format* check (gbXML shell vs. IFC4 space
   footprint/volume) rather than the same face-level checks. No such cross-check exists today.

The IFC4 artifact of the same model is **not** touched by these mutations and still passes
`validate_ifc4` (measured: `validate_ifc4('bem_out/sheet_007.ifc') -> PASS []`) — today's gate never
compares the two files, so a geometric defect in the gbXML leaves a "valid" IFC4 sitting next to it.


| # | seeded defect | how it was seeded | current `validate_gbxml` | new Part A checks | which Part A check fires |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **open shell** | deleted one wall `PolyLoop` from `sp-001` | **NOT CAUGHT (PASS)** | **CAUGHT** | closed shell: 4 edges not shared by 2 faces |
| 2 | **flipped normal** | reversed the winding of one wall face | **NOT CAUGHT (PASS)** | **CAUGHT** | winding: 4 edges traversed the same way twice |
| 3 | **non-planar quad** | raised one shell corner by **5 mm** in all 3 faces that carry it (shell stays closed) | **NOT CAUGHT (PASS)** | **CAUGHT** | planarity: worst **1.25e-03 m** > 1e-3 m; warp spread: **2.50e-03 m** > 9e-4 m |
| 4 | **duplicate surface** | duplicated envelope `<Surface id="wall-001b">` with a fresh `id` | **NOT CAUGHT (PASS)** | **NOT CAUGHT** | — (blind spot, see below) |
| 5 | **degenerate zero-area face** | added a face of 3 coincident points to `sp-001` | **NOT CAUGHT (PASS)** | **CAUGHT** | degenerate face: 1 |
| 6 | **negative-volume space** | inverted the winding of every face of `sp-001` | **NOT CAUGHT (PASS)** | **CAUGHT** | signed volume: min **−274.344 m³** (≤ 0) |
| 7 | *perturbed vertex* (extra) | moved one wall vertex 10 mm sideways | **NOT CAUGHT (PASS)** | **CAUGHT** | closed shell: 4 edges not shared; planarity 2.50e-03 m; warp 5.00e-03 m |
| 8 | *duplicate face* (extra) | duplicated one shell face (same vertices, same winding) | **NOT CAUGHT (PASS)** | **CAUGHT** | duplicate face: 1; closed shell: 4 edges not shared |

**Scorecard**

* Required seeds (1–6): **today's validators catch 0 of 6**; **the Part A checks catch 5 of 6**.
* With the two extra seeds: Part A catches **7 of 8**.

Three findings that change how Part A must be written:

1. **The volume-sign check alone is not enough for a flipped normal.** Seed 2 (one flipped wall face)
   leaves the space volume at **+42.46 m³** — still positive — so a `volume > 0` test would *pass* it.
   What catches it is the **edge-direction (winding) pair check** inside the closed-shell test. Seed 6
   (all faces inverted) is caught by the volume sign. Both sub-checks are needed.
2. **The Part A check set as specified is scoped to space shells and therefore misses a duplicated
   envelope surface** (seed 4). A duplicate `<Surface>` with a fresh `id` is XSD-valid, passes
   `_gbxml_semantic_checks` (ids are unique, refs intact) and is invisible to a space-shell audit.
   Part A needs one more check on the envelope `<Surface>` set (duplicate id / duplicate geometry /
   duplicate area) before it can be landed as a gate. Note also that when the duplicated surface *does*
   contain openings, the XSD fails only because the copied `<Opening>` re-uses an `xs:ID` — that is an
   id-collision artifact, not duplicate detection.
3. **The tolerances are not yet exercised by real geometry.** Every retrospective face deviation is
   exactly 0 (all faces are axis-aligned quads) and the smallest seeded planarity defect that the
   1e-3 m tolerance catches is 1.25e-03 m, i.e. a 5 mm corner raise. The 1e-3 m / 9e-4 m starting
   tolerances have **zero false positives on today's exports** (53/53 shells) and catch a 5 mm warp —
   but the flat-roof synth set does not exercise warped/sloped shells at all, so the warp tolerance is
   currently validated only against the seeded mutation, not against a real sloped-roof export.

## 4. Persistence demonstration (Gap A) — a corrupted export at its final path

Script `/tmp/1402/persist_demo.py` (throwaway). What it measured, verbatim:

**Step 1 — a normal run.** `bem_out/sheet_007.xml` exists, 85 510 bytes, `sha256=22332af2…`.

**Step 2 — the export FAILS validation (validator stubbed to fail), with the real writer in place:**

```
  run_bem_export.main returned False (False = the run reports failure)
  file existed at validation time: True (85510 bytes)
  after FAILED run: bem_out/sheet_007.xml exists, 85510 bytes, sha256=22332af2443fdee2
  => the artifact published by the FAILED run is still at its final path: True
```

i.e. **the file is written to its final path *before* validation runs**, and when validation fails the
run reports failure (exit code 1) but **leaves the artifact in place**. The gbXML writer itself is a
plain non-atomic write (`bem/bem_export.py:811`: `with open(path, "w", encoding="utf-8") as f: …`),
and the repo's `atomic_io.write_text_atomic` is **not** used on this path.

**Step 3 — a corrupted export sitting at its final path today (Gap A, on the record):**

```
  after truncation: bem_out/sheet_007.xml exists, 51306 bytes, sha256=4176b72dc51ae9da
  validate_gbxml -> FAIL; first error: not well-formed: expected '>', line 3, column 50287 (sheet_007.xml, line 3)
  after validation failed: bem_out/sheet_007.xml exists, 51306 bytes, sha256=4176b72dc51ae9da
  => the corrupt artifact is still at its final path: True
  => re-validating the same path still fails: True
```

The file was truncated in place (simulating a crash mid-write, or any bad publish) and then
**re-validated by the repo's own validator**: it fails, and it **stays at `bem_out/sheet_007.xml`** —
the final, consumable path. Nothing in the repo quarantines, renames or deletes it: `bem_out/` is
written by `write_gbxml`/`write_ifc4` (`bem/bem_export.py:811`, `bem/bem_ifc4.py:920`) and read back
only by the validators; there is no "publish" step, no staging directory and no atomic rename
(`grep -rn "publish" --include=*.py .` → no export-publishing code). `atomic_io.write_text_atomic`
exists and is used on other paths (`cli.py:456`, `run_pipeline.py:78`, `review/corrections.py:58`,
`legend_learning.py:89`) but is **not** called on the export path (`bem/bem_export.py`,
`bem/bem_ifc4.py`).

**Step 4 — the next successful run overwrites it:**

```
  after the next good run: bem_out/sheet_007.xml exists, 85510 bytes, sha256=22332af2443fdee2
  validate_gbxml -> PASS
```

So today's behaviour is *self-healing on the next run*, not *gated*: between the bad write and the
next good run, a consumer of `bem_out/` sees the corrupt file at its final path. That is precisely the
Gap A the issue names — the export is **published before it is proven good**.
