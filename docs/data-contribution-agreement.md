# Data-contribution agreement (template, v1.0)

This is the template for contributing drawings, IFC models, schedules,
or derived datasets to matchline's flywheel. Version 1.0 (2026-10-15);
later versions bump the version line and date, and a contribution is
governed by the version in force when it was accepted.

**This is a template, not legal advice.** Review it with counsel before
adopting it for real contributions.

## 1. Grant

The contributor grants the matchline project a perpetual, irrevocable,
worldwide license to use, reproduce, modify, and redistribute the
contributed data as part of matchline and its derivatives, under the
project's license terms. Where the contributor marks the data
noncommercial or share-alike, the license ledger (`matchline
license_ledger`, #751) records that marking, and noncommercial data is
used for evaluation only and never shipped in distributed artifacts.

## 2. Warranties

The contributor states that they have the right to contribute the data
and that, to their knowledge, it does not infringe others' rights.
The contributor is not warranted to be the data's original author;
they may contribute data they are licensed to share.

## 3. Privacy

Contributed data must not contain personal information (names,
addresses, signatures of identifiable individuals). Contributors are
responsible for redacting title blocks, stamps, and sheet borders
before submitting.

## 4. Attribution and versioning

Contributions are recorded in the license ledger with contributor,
date, license, and permitted use. The ledger is the agreement of
record; this template governs interpretation.

## 5. Withdrawal

A contributor may request removal of their data from future releases;
already-released versions keep their history. Evaluation results
derived from contributed data need not be retracted.
