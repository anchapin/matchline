# 1.0 accuracy claims

What 1.0 may say about accuracy: measured numbers only, never asserted
(1.0 gate #1165, ROADMAP promise 5). Every number below comes from the
2026-10-20 benchmark run recorded in
[DATASETS.md](../DATASETS.md#real-building-benchmark-748), scored against
frozen independent IFC truth (`truth.json`, sha256-pinned, produced by
`scripts/bench_truth.py`), with a convention report written per building.

**Scope.** 17 configured buildings, 17 scored (develop `fd74833`, 2026-10-20,
rerun after the #1204/#1205/#1206 fixes and the #1215 import guard). The
esplanades, duplex and molio rows were re-measured 2026-10-21 on the #1217
kernel-units fix and #1218's truth floor-level fix (duplex and molio truth
re-frozen for it). The
wbdg-office row is included; its license was confirmed 2026-10-20 under the
Common BIM Files reuse clause with the ERDC-CERL / buildingSMART alliance
citation (see DATASETS.md; a different NIBS answer governs and the row is
revisited). molio needs `MATCHLINE_MAX_IFC_ELEMENTS=1500000`.

**Measured per-slice accuracy (17 buildings, no `--openstudio` run yet):**

- Rooms: 1,304 of 1,323 truth spaces recovered (98.6%). Paired room area
  error: median 0.0004%, max 1.3% (molio). The duplex outlier (109.7%
  before #1204, 45.3% after) fell to 0.0004% with the #1218 truth
  floor-level fix; every building is now within 1.4%.
- Exterior wall length error: median 23.6%, range 0.0% to 93.5%, over the 14
  buildings whose IFC flags walls as external. Published as a spread, not a
  single number: molio (93.5%) is the open defect. The esplanades outlier
  (86.6%) was fixed by the #1217 kernel-units work and now reads 8.2%. The
  gni-49 outlier (~800%) was fixed by the wall-length body-geometry and
  direct-fill work (#1205/#1206) and now reads 31.8%.
- Windows: 1,410 of 1,423 truth windows recovered (99.1%). Window-area error:
  median 0.09%, range 0.0% to 930.5%; the gni-49 outlier (defective window
  placements in the file itself) is an open defect, not a claim. The
  esplanades (~100%) and molio (~100%) outliers were fixed by the #1217
  kernel-units work and now read 0.4% and 0.0%.
- Doors: 1,147 of 1,155 truth doors recovered (99.3%).

**Not claimed.** LPD accuracy has no independent truth yet. OpenStudio
end-to-end simulation and the drawing cross-frontend check were not exercised
in the scored run. Until those run, 1.0 materials make no claim about them.

Reproduce: `python scripts/benchmark.py --config scripts/benchmarks.json
--out scoreboard.md --json scoreboard.json` (see DATASETS.md for the dataset
layout, licenses and the truth-freeze step).
