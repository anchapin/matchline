# 1.0 accuracy claims

What 1.0 may say about accuracy: measured numbers only, never asserted
(1.0 gate #1165, ROADMAP promise 5). Every number below comes from the
2026-10-21 benchmark run recorded in
[DATASETS.md](../DATASETS.md#real-building-benchmark-748), scored against
frozen independent IFC truth (`truth.json`, sha256-pinned, produced by
`scripts/bench_truth.py`, schema `matchline.bench_truth/2`: window area is
read from the window's tessellated body per #1220), with a convention report
written per building.

**Scope.** 17 configured buildings, 17 scored (develop `4162adf`, 2026-10-21,
full rerun after the #1219/#1227/#1229 wall-length and space-footprint placement
fixes, with all 17 truths re-frozen under the `matchline.bench_truth/2`
schema). The
wbdg-office row is included; its license was confirmed 2026-10-20 under the
Common BIM Files reuse clause with the ERDC-CERL / buildingSMART alliance
citation (see DATASETS.md; a different NIBS answer governs and the row is
revisited). molio needs `MATCHLINE_MAX_IFC_ELEMENTS=1500000`.

**Measured per-slice accuracy (17 buildings, no `--openstudio` run yet):**

- Rooms: 1,304 of 1,323 truth spaces recovered (98.6%). Paired room area
  error: median 0.0004%, max 1.3% (molio). The duplex outlier (109.7%
  before #1204, 45.3% after) fell to 0.0004% with the #1218 truth
  floor-level fix; every building is now within 1.4%.
- Exterior wall length error: median 0.2%, range 0.0% to 1.7%, over the 14
  buildings whose IFC flags walls as external. Published as a spread, not a
  single number. The molio outlier (93.5%) was fixed by the #1219 mm-scale
  and pset-exterior work and now reads 0.1%; esplanades (fixed by the #1217
  kernel-units work and the #1229 space-footprint placement fix) reads 0.4%;
  gni-49 (fixed by the #1205/#1206 wall-length work) reads 1.1%; smiley-west
  dropped from 9.7% to 1.7% with the #1229 and #1227 fixes. The last two
  outliers, gni-136 (23.0%) and digital-hub (17.8%), fell to 0.5% and 0.7%
  with the #1227 declared-data-first wall ladder: the import now reads
  Qto_WallBaseQuantities, then the Axis polyline, then the body, the same
  ladder the truth builder uses, instead of taking an axis-aligned body
  bbox's length, which mislabels the thickness as the length for walls whose
  profile is not x-aligned.
- Windows: 1,410 of 1,423 truth windows recovered (99.1%). Window-area error:
  median 0.7%, range 0.0% to 14.0%. The 930.5% gni-49 outlier was a truth
  artifact: the file's stated window attributes are corrupt (frame thickness
  in the width field, inconsistently swapped), and #1220 now reads window
  truth from the window's tessellated body. gni-49 reads 14.0% (62 of 71
  windows recovered against body-measured truth). The esplanades (100%) and
  molio (100%) outliers were fixed by the #1217 kernel-units work; under the
  /2 geometry-derived truths esplanades reads 4.9% and molio 0.0%.
- Doors: 1,147 of 1,155 truth doors recovered (99.3%).

**Not claimed.** LPD accuracy has no independent truth yet. OpenStudio
end-to-end simulation and the drawing cross-frontend check were not exercised
in the scored run. Until those run, 1.0 materials make no claim about them.

Reproduce: `python scripts/benchmark.py --config scripts/benchmarks.json
--out scoreboard.md --json scoreboard.json` (see DATASETS.md for the dataset
layout, licenses and the truth-freeze step).

## Mechanical schedule extraction (#1240)

Measured 2026-10-22 on the clinic's COBie workbook
(`clinic-design.xlsx`), scored against frozen independent schedule truth
(`truth.json` next to the workbook, produced by `scripts/schedule_truth.py`,
schema `matchline.schedule_truth/1`, workbook sha256-pinned; the truth
reader imports openpyxl only, never the extractor, so the run measures
extraction rather than self-comparison). Run:
`python scripts/schedule_bench.py --building bsi-clinic`.

- Identity: 1,520 truth-tagged component rows (242 duplicate tags recorded).
  The extractor emits 1,062 rows: 410 unique tags, every one a truth tag
  (precision 1.000), plus 591 rows that parse to no tag and 61 duplicate-tag
  rows. Recall 0.270.
- What recall misses: 555 `Equip-*` tags (A1155, A5145, A1066, D8850 —
  equipment families the extractor's text rules exclude), 469 light-fixture
  and 71 window tags (excluded by design), and a handful of sink,
  receptacle and expansion-tank rows. The exclusions are stated, never
  hidden by the score.
- Zone linkage: 410 of 410 matched tags carry a service area equal to the
  truth's stated Space reference.
- Stated values: 267 matched tags state a capacity token; the extractor's
  capacity agrees with 3 of 267 (the stated "2500 Watt" family is not in
  its unit table, and the value-slot ladder routed 2,740 stated values to
  review with 0 slotted on this workbook).
- Airflow: the workbook states 33 airflow requirements on its Attribute
  sheet; the extractor reads the Type sheet only, so 0 are recovered.
  Measured gap, tracked as follow-up work.
- Scores over the stated fields (service area, capacity): grouped 0.096,
  flat F1 0.309, KR 0.751, EM 0.096, VF1 0.309. The grouped-vs-flat gap is
  capacity: service areas agree, capacities do not.
- Export validity: the clinic model's BuildingSync export passes XSD
  validation (measured 2026-10-22, `--export-validity`).
