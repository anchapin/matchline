# Risk register

Top risks to the matchline project, reviewed whenever a new release or a
gating dependency changes. Likelihood and impact are qualitative (low /
medium / high). Owners: "Alex" for human decisions and hardware, "Fo"
for automation upkeep the aggressive loop runs.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|------|-----------|--------|------------|-------|
| 1 | OpenStudio wheels gap: openstudio 3.11.0 segfaults under CPython 3.15, so CI pins `python < 3.15` via the Renovate rule (#969) until NREL ships cp315 wheels (upstream NREL/OpenStudio issue #5668) | Medium | Medium | Pin holds dependency PRs until wheels exist; the Renovate `python` rule auto-reopens the question each Monday window. Track #5668 and lift the pin in the same commit that bumps openstudio | Fo tracks upstream; Alex approves the bump |
| 2 | Real-building benchmark stalls on licensing: #748's results wait on licensed building data; noncommercial / copy-left datasets (AEC-Geometric-Bench, CC BY-NC 4.0) are eval-only and NC weights stay quarantined per #751 | Medium | High | License ledger (`matchline.license_ledger`) gates every new dataset before use; permissive/share-alike ships, NC is eval-only; held-out IFCs never committed | Alex adopts data; Fo keeps the ledger current |
| 3 | Single GPU runner: one machine (`alex-system`, RX 6600 XT, ROCm) carries all training and eval; the Clinic IFC is not present on the runner, so dispatches must stay eval-only and runs serialize | High | Medium | Eval-only dispatches only (never re-dispatch training); conf sweep stays at or above dump conf (0.05); training data stays on the runner; anything over 200 epochs needs Alex's approval first | Alex (hardware, approvals); Fo (dispatch discipline) |
| 4 | Detector accuracy plateau: thin walls 3-7 px apart are the known false-positive root cause (corrected-truth hypothesis refuted); further gains need deliberate experiments, not prompt-driven churn | Medium | Medium | Experiments run one lever at a time against the eval harness; specialists frozen pending the chosen lever; keep the runner-up lever queued rather than switching mid-run | Fo (experiments); Alex (approves long runs, picks levers) |
| 5 | Review-queue gate weakens silently: shipped exports depend on unacknowledged review items blocking export; auto-triage widening or a validation-battery relaxation would let wrong models through | Low | High | Gate is input-agnostic and tested (drawing and IFC paths both stop at it); any change to triage confidence or gate checks lands as a reviewed PR with a failing-then-passing test | Fo |
| 6 | Test-count discipline drifts: `EXPECTED_TEST_COUNT` must move in the same commit as any test change; an off-by-one leaves develop's count check red (happened once: #990 merged with pin 3240 vs true 3241) | Medium | Low | Count verified locally with `pytest --collect-only` before every push; a mismatch is investigated, never hand-waved; docs/ci.md kept in sync | Fo |
| 7 | PDF library contamination: merging/reading PDFs through an AGPL library (e.g. PyMuPDF) would force licensing decisions the project does not want | Low | Medium | pypdfium2 is the only PDF entry point (Apache-2.0/BSD-3); no second PDF library is added without a license decision in an ADR | Fo |
| 8 | Automation-scoped credentials: the GitHub token is scoped to the project's own repos, so upstream work (filing on NREL/OpenStudio, searching other orgs) runs through different channels and can look like a capability gap when it is only a scope line | Medium | Low | Upstream filings go through the MCP GitHub route with the user's account; scope limits are reported as scope limits, not outages | Fo |
| 9 | Local-only steps lost on restart: training data, the ROCm venv, and C compilation artifacts live on the runner and under `/workspace`-adjacent paths that a restart can wipe (`/tmp` is always disposable) | Medium | Medium | Datasets live under durable paths (`/workspace/workspace/datasets/matchline-bench/`); anything that must survive a restart is re-checkable from the repo or the license ledger, never from `/tmp` | Alex (runner); Fo (staging discipline) |
| 10 | Renovate window churn: dependency PRs only open "before 6am on Monday"; out-of-window bumps get missed or done by hand and drift from the schedule | Low | Low | Urgent bumps stay exception-driven with a note in the PR; #680 (Dependency Dashboard) closes only when nothing is pending, so drift is visible on the board | Fo |
